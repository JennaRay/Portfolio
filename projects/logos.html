i-Hack Logo

BYU i-Hack is a competition where students set out in small teams to come up with unique solutions to problems using technology and programming. I created this logo for the event in 2023. The logo features a stylized "iH" reminiscent of a circuit board pattern. The gradient of blue represents the technology aspect of the event, and the font is modern and dynamic to represent the innovation and creativity of the students involved in the competition.
