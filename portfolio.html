i-Hack Logo

BYU-I's "I-Hack" is a competition where students set out in small teams to come up with unique solutions to problems using technology and programming. I created this logo for the event in 2023. The logo features a stylized "IH" reminiscent of a circuit board pattern. The gradient of blue represents the technology aspect of the event, and the font is modern and dynamic to represent the innovation and creativity of the students involved in the competition.

SWERL Logo

SWERL is a program that takes features from swift and erlang that each language is individually known for and combines them. To illustrate this, I borrowed the logos of swift and erlang and put them together in a swirling, yin-yang form, showing the two different ideas working together to form something new.

Beach Murder Mystery Board Game

This board game design was commissioned by Jonas, who wanted to make a unique board game for his family who likes to play clue together when they vacation at the beach. I wanted to keep the iconic clue aesthetic with the birdseye view of each room in the house, and the posh manor feel, but also add a beachy spin. Each room is also based off of actual rooms in the beach house that Jonas's family frequents, and there are multiple easter eggs in each design that relate to his family's fond memories there.

Nature Board Game

Player cards

For this nature themed board game I was heavily inspired by the aesthetics of field journals/field guides. The papery and leathery textures on the cards, the dark inky details, and the hand drawn images of the player cards are all meant to evoke the feeling of those well-worn books.
