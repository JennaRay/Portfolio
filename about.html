About Me

Hi! I'm Jenna Ray, a graphic designer and illustrator based in Oregon. I love creating visual stories and bringing ideas to life. I specialize in branding, logo design, and digital illustration. I'm passionate about helping businesses and individuals communicate their message through beautiful and effective design. When I'm not designing, you can find me making music, reading, or playing with my cat, Taffy.
